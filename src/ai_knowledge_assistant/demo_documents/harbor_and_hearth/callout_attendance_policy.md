# Harbor & Hearth Café Call-Out and Attendance Policy

## Calling out

If a team member cannot work a scheduled shift, they must call the on-duty shift
lead at least two hours before the shift starts. A text message, social-media
message, or message through another team member does not replace the call.

For an emergency that makes two hours’ notice impossible, call the shift lead as
soon as it is safe to do so and explain that the situation is an emergency.

## Follow-up

The team member should state their name, scheduled start time, and whether they
expect to miss the full shift. The shift lead records the call-out in the daily
manager log. Team members must not arrange an unapproved replacement and assume
the shift is covered.
