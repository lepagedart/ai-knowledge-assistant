# Harbor & Hearth Café Menu and Product Reference

## Allergen information

The allergen guide lists ingredients supplied by vendors, but the cafe cannot
guarantee an allergen-free environment because equipment and service areas are
shared. Never state that an item is safe for a guest with an allergy.

## Allergen concern escalation

If a guest reports an allergen concern, stop preparing or serving the affected
item and notify the shift lead immediately. Preserve the order ticket and note
the item, requested modification, and stated concern. The shift lead reviews the
allergen guide and contacts the manager for further direction. Do not provide
medical advice.

## Substitutions

Team members may explain listed ingredient substitutions but must check with the
shift lead when a request conflicts with an allergen note or an out-of-stock
notice.
