# Harbor & Hearth Café New Team Member Training Guide

## First-week checklist

New team members complete an orientation, review the Employee Handbook, shadow
an opening or closing shift, and practice register sign-in with a trainer. A
shift lead signs the checklist after each activity is observed.

## Guest-service practice

Training includes listening to guest concerns, locating the written policy, and
escalating refund, safety, and allergy concerns to the shift lead. Trainees may
not approve refunds or make allergen-safety claims.

## Readiness review

At the end of the first week, the shift lead reviews attendance expectations,
cash handling, product-reference use, and escalation steps with the trainee.
Additional coaching is scheduled when an observed task is incomplete.
