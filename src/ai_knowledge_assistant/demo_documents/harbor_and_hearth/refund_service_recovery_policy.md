# Harbor & Hearth Café Refund and Service Recovery Policy

## First response

Thank the guest, listen to the concern, and notify the shift lead before
promising a refund, replacement, or discount. Record the order details and the
guest’s stated concern in the daily service-recovery log.

## Refund approval

The shift lead may approve a full refund for an incorrect or unsafe item after
reviewing the order. Refunds above $25.00, repeated refund requests, and any
allergen-related concern require manager review before a refund is processed.
The manager records the final decision and the reason in the log.

## Safety concerns

For a possible allergen issue, stop service of the affected item, preserve the
order details, and follow the allergen escalation steps in the Menu and Product
Reference. Team members do not give medical advice or guarantee a refund.
