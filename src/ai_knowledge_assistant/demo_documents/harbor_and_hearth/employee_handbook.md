# Harbor & Hearth Café Employee Handbook

## Purpose and conduct

Harbor & Hearth Café is a fictional neighborhood cafe used only for this demo.
Team members greet guests warmly, communicate respectfully, and ask a shift lead
for help when a policy or guest concern is unclear.

## Scheduling and breaks

Schedules are posted by Thursday for the following week. Team members take one
paid 10-minute rest break on shifts of four to five hours and one unpaid
30-minute meal break on shifts longer than six hours. The shift lead confirms
the timing of breaks so service remains covered.

## Guest concerns

Listen without arguing, thank the guest for raising the concern, and involve the
shift lead for refund, safety, or allergy-related concerns. Do not promise an
outcome outside the written policy.
