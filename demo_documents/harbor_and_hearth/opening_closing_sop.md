# Harbor & Hearth Café Opening and Closing SOP

## Opening the cash drawer

1. The opener and shift lead count the cash drawer together before the cafe
   opens. The opening float must equal $200.00.
2. Record the counted total on the daily cash log and both people initial it.
3. If the count differs from $200.00, do not adjust it. Notify the shift lead,
   record the difference, and wait for their direction.
4. Sign in to the register with the opener profile only after the count is
   recorded. Keep the drawer closed except during a transaction.

## Opening readiness

Unlock the front entrance at the scheduled opening time, confirm the espresso
machine is warmed, and review the day’s allergen and out-of-stock notes during
the pre-shift huddle.

## Closing

Two team members count the drawer, complete the closing cash log, secure cash in
the labeled deposit envelope, and lock it in the office safe. The shift lead
confirms doors, refrigeration checks, and the alarm before leaving.
